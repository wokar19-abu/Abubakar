The Greens Prom

Seeta High School Green Campus

10th, June 2025

The Greens Prom was the legendary 2026 candidate classes prom, involving both the S.4s and S.6s of Seeta Green.

It was organised by candidate class teachers, led by the legendary Mr. Kizito Isaac Lesage, who organised an unforgettable and nostalgic day for all candidates.

As the Seeta culture implies, all students arrived at school the day before the prom day, so as to prepare for the seeta traditional kasiki party, in the night where they were served a satisfying dish to give them some energy for the Kasiki Kadanke, hosted by DJ Tus and Laycon Daga.

Finally, on the big day, since Seeta always puts God first, the day started with a Holy Mass, where people decided to start getting out their solid outfits, as some appeared in Turkish two piece suits, others in South American gentleman casulas and the girls flittered in their perfect body huggers.

Deeper into the day, an entertainment session was arranged where people decided to dress up street style, or what planned kids were calling "KIKUMBA STYLE". During this period , people interracted with some music and Breakfast bangers.

After a Lunch meal, the planned kids' favourite part was initiated, the cocktail party, which had an old skul brown theme, where people enjoyed cocktail drinks while being enetertained by the talented Winnie Nawagi, a famous Ugandan Female artiste.

Lastly, the day ended with a majestic Red Carpet Walk and Dinner party, under a Black and White Theme, where Boys rocked in all luxurious and expensive suits as the enjoyed the night with their beautiful prom dates who were looking stunning in their blazzind huggers surrounded by all sorts of jewelwry.

 

Wild Nyt

Seeta High School Green Campus

10th, June 2025

The Wild Nyt was simply the After prom party which involved ony The Boys, as the girls were sent off back to thier wing.

The dinner started at about 6:30 pm, and stretched up to 10:30pm,where girls were sent off allowing boys to release the inner beasts in them to party.

During the dinner, all boys were playing cool kid vibes as they acted as gentlemen so as to impress their dates.

At the end of the dinner, all boys dressed their coats around their gals to show care and gentility, but when they left, beasts were set free.

All boys looked and grabbed the closest wine bottle, and started drinking while clubbing to the music, showing how wild they are. Some even claim it was the best night og their lives.

The best moment was when everyone vibed to the old tune Smart wire, each with a wine bottle in thier hands, with their ties wrapped arounf their heads.
